What are the main energy efficiency and cost arguments for and against using natural ventilation in a modern energy-efficient building (for example, a nearly zero-energy house)?

For which type of building (by purpose, size, location) is simple but uncontrolled natural ventilation still more suitable?

How can the advantages of both systems be combined — is there a way for natural ventilation to work as a supplementary or seasonal solution to a mechanical system?

 

Topic 2: Heat recovery — does it always pay off?

At what point does installing a heat recovery unit become economically justified? Discuss taking into account: building size, heat prices, electricity price, system complexity.

What is the hierarchy of heat recovery technologies (counterflow, rotary, liquid loop) in terms of efficiency, cost and maintenance? Which would you choose for a school and which for a factory workshop?

What are the biggest operating challenges for heat recovery units in Latvian climate conditions (freezing, condensate, cleaning)? How can they be avoided?

 

Topic 3: Apartment building — ventilation policy and individual responsibility

What is the optimal ventilation solution for an apartment building: a centralised system with heat recovery or individual room units (decentralised ventilation)? Discuss from the point of view of the apartment owner, the building manager and the energy auditor.

How can the conflict between the need for fresh air and the wish to save heat be resolved when some residents constantly open windows and others do not? Is there a “socially acceptable” ventilation regime?

What would be the fairest and most effective way of metering heat energy in a building with centralised ventilation?

 

Topic 4: Industrial ventilation — safety, energy and the environment

What are the role and limits of local exhaust (extraction above process equipment) compared with general ventilation? When is each more effective?

What is the main trade-off when designing ventilation for a factory: maximum air quality (large flows) or minimum energy consumption? Is there a “golden mean”?

How can the cleaning of polluted air (filters, scrubbers) be combined with energy-saving measures? Should all the air be cleaned in large volumes, or should the pollution be captured effectively at the source locally?

 

Topic 5: Air quality — invisible threats and their control

What practical level of air quality monitoring is sufficient in an office building? Is a CO₂ sensor enough, or are VOC and PM2.5 sensors also needed? Which parameter is the most informative indicator?

How can building operators be motivated to clean and replace filters regularly if the direct consequences (poor well-being) are cumulative and unnoticeable?

Can the problem of volatile organic compounds (VOC) in homes be solved effectively by ventilation alone, or are special filters (carbon filters) also essential? Discuss in relation to new houses and renovated apartments.

 

Topic 6: The future of smart ventilation — digital control and “greenwashing”

How much energy saving potential lies in switching from constant-flow ventilation to a demand-controlled (DCV) system? Which spaces (schools, theatres, supermarkets) could benefit the most?

What are the essential differences between a fan switched on by a simple timer and a truly adaptive system that integrates data from a sensor network, the weather forecast and a schedule?

When does a ventilation solution become too complex (“overengineering”)?
