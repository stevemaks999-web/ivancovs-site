Adjusting and maintaining windows and balcony (loggia) and terrace doors is the Buyer's responsibility and is not covered by the warranty.

? Replacing the filters of the ventilation unit, servicing the unit and setting individual ventilation settings are the responsibility of the apartment owner and are not covered by the warranty terms. Repair or replacement of faulty equipment and/or parts does not extend the warranty period of the ventilation unit. During the warranty period, only original manufacturer filters may be installed.

The warranty terms for the ventilation unit are not valid if:
- The unit has been damaged through carelessness or improper operation (for example, mechanical damage, etc.)
- The unit is used contrary to the operating and/or maintenance instructions;
- Damage has been found in the unit, but it continues to be used without regard to the damage;
- Unsuitable parts are used in the unit (for example, adapted spare parts, filters, etc.)
- For any reason the Buyer denies access to the premises and/or the ventilation system to the extent required for warranty service.

? The warranty terms and liability do not apply to damage arising in the following cases:
• Improper operation of the ventilation unit or control devices;
• incorrect or unsuitable installation, setup or operation;
• failure to follow transport, installation, operating or maintenance instructions;
• structural or electronic modifications or changes made to the software.
